retrying a failed key request, expired invites, single-use enforcement,
concurrent redemption, stable request-ID retries and bearer authentication.

the gate, verifies doctor, opens MCP using only saved configuration, preserves

installation's reuse, stops and restarts the gate, and checks malformed settings.

late votes, legacy-state migration, delivery interruptions, unavailable home
notifications and the single gate-process lock. Fact/trust approval scenarios
terminate subprocesses after profile, trust and ledger writes, then recover one
decision and one approval record.

The installation runner builds a wheel and installs it into a clean environment
outside the checkout. Its six checks cover imports, share cards, the wake CLI,
watcher prompts, the packaged exporter and the missing-profile error.
